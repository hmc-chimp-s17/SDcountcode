================================================================
Comparison: v7 (s1-t1 any cycles) vs v8 (s1-t1 exclusive cycles)
================================================================

VERSION OVERVIEW:
-----------------

v7: s1-t1 path with >=1 cycles (no restrictions on s2-t3 or s3-t2)
v8: s1-t1 path with >=1 cycles AND s2-t3 with 0 cycles AND s3-t2 with 0 cycles

Both versions:
- Merge vertices: a1,a2,a3→a; b1,b2,b3→b; c1,c2,c3→c
- Split a-c edges: each a-c edge becomes a-b and b-c
- Exclude self-loops from cycle counting

================================================================
RESULTS COMPARISON:
================================================================

6J Configuration:
-----------------
v7 (s1-t1 any):              61 total (52 non-special + 9 special)
v8 (s1-t1 exclusive):        43 total (39 non-special + 4 special)
Difference:                  18 matchings (13 non-special + 5 special)

PILLOW Configuration:
---------------------
v7 (s1-t1 any):              58 total (52 non-special + 6 special)
v8 (s1-t1 exclusive):        44 total (40 non-special + 4 special)
Difference:                  14 matchings (12 non-special + 2 special)

================================================================
KEY INSIGHTS:
================================================================

1. Exclusive s1-t1 cycles (v8) represent ~70% of all s1-t1 cycles (v7):
   - 6J:    43/61 = 70.5%
   - PILLOW: 44/58 = 75.9%

2. About 25-30% of matchings with s1-t1 cycles ALSO have cycles in 
   other paths (s2-t3 or s3-t2):
   - 6J:    18/61 = 29.5%
   - PILLOW: 14/58 = 24.1%

3. For non-special matchings:
   - 6J:    39/52 = 75.0% are exclusive to s1-t1
   - PILLOW: 40/52 = 76.9% are exclusive to s1-t1

4. For special matchings:
   - 6J:    4/9 = 44.4% are exclusive to s1-t1
   - PILLOW: 4/6 = 66.7% are exclusive to s1-t1

5. Special matchings show higher overlap with other paths than
   non-special matchings, particularly for 6J.

================================================================
INTERPRETATION:
================================================================

v8 identifies matchings where cycles appear EXCLUSIVELY in the s1-t1 
path after vertex merging and a-c splitting. This is a stricter subset
of v7, filtering out cases where multiple paths have cycles.

This could be important for physics applications where you want to 
study contributions that affect only one specific path topology.

The ~70-75% exclusivity rate suggests that most s1-t1 cycles are 
independent of the other paths, but a significant minority (~25-30%) 
occur in conjunction with cycles in s2-t3 or s3-t2.
